Philosophy

Built like software
used to be.

Three principles, on every screen.

Native, not a tab.

Built with SwiftUI from the first pixel. Launches in a blink, respects your menu bar, plays nicely with Spotlight.

Private by default.

Everything lives in Core Data on your Mac. No cloud, no analytics, no account. Encrypt your expenses behind a PIN.

Yours, forever.

One-time license. Pay once, own it. Export anything you've made any time. Nothing to renew, nothing to lose.
