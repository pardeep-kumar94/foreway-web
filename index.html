FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Mind Anchor is a free all-in-one productivity app for macOS that brings goal tracking, daily habit building, personal finance management, task planning, deadline tracking, and journaling into a single, focused workspace. It replaces five or more scattered apps with one calm, native Mac experience.

Most productivity tools are single-purpose — one app for goals, another for habits, another for finance. Mind Anchor combines all of them into one seamless workspace, so you never lose context by switching apps. It's also 100% local with no subscription, unlike cloud-based competitors.

Mind Anchor is completely free to download and use — no subscription, no in-app purchases, no paywalls. Every feature is available from day one at no cost.

Never. Mind Anchor is 100% local. Your goals, habits, financial data, and journal entries live exclusively on your Mac. No accounts required, no data ever sent to a server.

Mind Anchor runs on macOS 13 Ventura or later and is optimised natively for Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) as well as Intel Macs.

Yes. The Finance Dashboard tracks income, expenses, savings accounts, investments, bills, and gives you a 6-month cash flow chart — all in one view, all locally on your Mac. No bank integrations required.
